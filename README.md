## 💡 Behavioral Interview Preparation

To succeed in behavioral interviews, familiarize yourself with the STAR method for structuring your responses:
*   **Situation (S):** Begin by describing the specific situation or context you were in to set the stage for your story.
*   **Task (T):** Explain the task or challenge you were faced with and the goals you needed to achieve.
*   **Action (A):** Describe the actions you took to address the challenge, focusing on your specific responsibilities and actions rather than the team's actions.
*   **Result (R):** Share the outcomes and impact of your actions, being quantitative whenever possible.
